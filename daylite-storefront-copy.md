## Hero

**Made in Africa, a proudly Namibian service.**

## Welcome to Daylite Digital

Daylite Digital is a digital business solution developed by Southern Cross Stars Investments CC, a member of Southern Cross Stars Group. This demonstration shows a digital commerce architecture that can represent destinations in Namibia and destinations elsewhere in the world.

## What the demonstration shows

AI discovery → controlled catalogue → server-authoritative pricing → PayPal Sandbox payment → verified confirmation.

## About the catalogue

The catalogue is demonstration data. It includes Namibian experiences alongside entries such as New York and Paris to show what the architecture can represent. It does not represent live inventory, live reservations or live international operations, and payments run in PayPal Sandbox only.
